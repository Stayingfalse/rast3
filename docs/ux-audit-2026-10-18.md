# UX audit, 18 October 2026 (Day 5)

**Scope.** Every page on dev.raosanta.uk, in four states: signed out, a new
member, a member with gifts, and a site admin. Each was captured at desktop
(1280×800) and on a Pixel 7.

**Method.** I looked at every capture and scanned each page with axe-core
(WCAG 2 A/AA plus best practice). Raw results: 38 page captures, 11 axe rule
types.

**Note on the date.** This runs after the 2026 redesign, brand mark, stamp
album and share cards, so it covers what members see now.

Before screenshots are in `e2e-screenshots/2026-10-18/audit/` (links below).
After screenshots are linked from the PR.

## Summary

The public site and the member home are in good shape after the redesign.
The problems are at the edges: old pages that weren't restyled, one real
privacy leak, a few accessibility failures that affect everyone on phones,
and an admin area that still looks and behaves like 2025.

## What works well
- **First viewport.** The landing page explains the idea before the fold on
  both sizes. Join Santa is the single red action, and the page doesn't
  scroll sideways at any width from 320 to 1440px.
- **Signed-in flow.** "Pick up your first three", then parcel labels, then
  Gifted, the share prompt and the stamp album read as one story. Every
  state is named in words, not only colour.
- **Dialogs.** Purchase, report, sign-in and share dialogs sit at the bottom
  of the screen on phones within thumb reach, close with Escape, and are
  never covered by the cookie strip.
- **Errors.** No JavaScript errors on any page in any state.

## Recommendations for the plan
1. **Day 6 (tonight, security):** remove or admin-gate `/success`,
   `/debug` and `/pwa-test`. `/success` leaks names today, so do it
   first.
2. **An admin day**, suggested for the spare day or merged into Day 13 (group
   admin console). That console will replace these pages anyway, so build it in
   the Christmas Post style from the start rather than restyling the old pages
   twice:
   - one `main` landmark;
   - a full-height sidebar, or tabs on phones;
   - users as a searchable, paged table with destructive actions behind a
     confirm.
3. **Day 29 (privacy and legal):** a real privacy policy covering share cards,
   photos, the stamp album, analytics and affiliate links (2027). You'll need
   to supply or approve the wording.
4. **Onboarding (Week 4):** profile setup copy that explains *why* each field
   matters, and making the wishlist required to receive.
