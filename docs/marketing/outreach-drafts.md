## 3. Founder post for remote-work communities

For remote-work Slack and Discord communities, newsletters that take reader
submissions, and forums where the rules allow a "show what you made" post.
Check each community's rules first.

**Title:** I built a free way for remote teams to send each other anonymous Christmas gifts

> My team is spread across the country, so the usual office Secret Santa
> never worked: nobody knows anyone's address, and a draw means someone
> always gets forgotten.
>
> So I built Random Acts of Santa (raosanta.uk). Everyone shares an Amazon
> wishlist. Whenever you're ready, you pick up a random colleague's list
> and send them something; Amazon delivers, so nobody sees an address.
> There's no draw: you can gift one person or five, and the people with the
> fewest gifts come up first, so nobody's left out. When something arrives,
> people post an anonymous thank-you.
>
> It's free, and it works for Discord servers and friends groups too.
> Almost 300 people have used it so far. Gifting is open until 6 January.
>
> I'd love feedback, especially from anyone running a remote team.
