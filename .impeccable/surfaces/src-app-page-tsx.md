# Home (/) surface brief

Scope: the public home page (Persuade) for signed-out visitors, and the same
route's signed-in view (Operate: wishlist assignments + thank-you feed), plus the
shared chrome they sit in (ground, header baubles, wordmark, sign-in, cookie notice).

Audience: workplaces first (colleagues, often remote), communities second.
Job: understand Random Acts (no draw, gift whenever you like, address never
shared) and join. Proof: live anonymised totals (gifts posted, thank-yous,
members), hidden when small. Constraints: keep the name, 2026 year mark, navy
night sky and bauble illustrations; grown-up, inclusive Christmas.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Random Acts as the Christmas post: every gift is a parcel sent to someone whose address you never learn. Refuses the category default of a snowy hero over three emoji benefit cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night-navy sky as ground, the incumbent hanging baubles above. Working surfaces are cream label stock with hairline ink rules, perforated stamp edges and a red/navy airmail band; postal red is the one action colour, gold is reserved for postmark rings. Archivo across widths: expanded caps for the franked wordmark and labels, regular for reading; tabular numerals for counts.

STORY: A visitor sees a parcel label addressed "To: a colleague · From: Santa · Address: withheld", understands that's the whole idea, sees the franking counter proving people do it, and joins.
